Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Islamic Baby Clothes ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This notice explains what information we collect when you use this website and place an order, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Order information: when you check out, our payment processor Stripe collects your name, email, phone number, shipping address and payment details. We receive your order, contact and shipping details so we can make and deliver your order. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your shopping bag: items you add to your bag are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so they're still there when you come back. They are not sent to us until you check out.
  • Messages: if you call, text or message us on Facebook, we receive the information you choose to share.

How we use it

We use your information to make, ship and support your order, to contact you about it, to answer your questions and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe processes payments (see Stripe's privacy policy).
  • Shipping carriers such as USPS receive your name and address to deliver your order.
  • Service providers that host this website (GitHub, Cloudflare) process basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by calling (313) 455-0054 or messaging us on Facebook. You can clear your saved bag at any time by emptying it or clearing your browser data.

Children

We make clothing for children, but this website is meant for parents and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
